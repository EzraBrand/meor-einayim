Azariah de' Rossi, Me'or Einayim

A Hebrew and English side-by-side text · pilot · מאור עינים לר' עזריה מן האדומים

Azariah de' Rossi (about 1511 to 1578) printed Me'or Einayim in Mantua in 1573–75. Its third and largest part, Imrei Binah, has 60 chapters of critical studies on rabbinic chronology and aggadah.

Sources

  • Hebrew text: the Vilna 1863–66 printing in the series Kiryat Sefer, begun by Isaac Benjacob (d. 1863), "printed after the Mantua edition, with all the first and second versions in their places in the text" (title page) (archive.org scan). The text here starts from the archive.org OCR and is corrected against the page images.
  • First edition: Mantua 1573–75 (National Library of Israel scan). Not yet compared.
  • Not included: the notes in Rashi type. By the title page they are "many additions by the author and some later scholars", until then in manuscript in the margin of a copy of the book owned by Leopold Zunz. This edition does not yet separate the author's notes from the later ones.

Translations that exist

  • Complete: Joanna Weinberg, The Light of the Eyes (Yale Judaica Series 31, Yale University Press, 2001), with introduction and annotations. It is in copyright and was not used for this draft.
  • Chapter 16: a Latin version in Samuel Bochart, Hierozoicon, and an English version by Morris Raphall in the Hebrew Review, vol. 2 (as listed in the Jewish Encyclopedia). Not yet compared.
